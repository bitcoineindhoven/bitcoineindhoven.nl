Meetup #8 · Reboot

Bitcoin Eindhoven is back, with a new home at PHOOD Farm

12 June 2026 · 17:00–22:00PHOOD Farm, Eindhoven

After a short pause, we're back. With Place du Nord closed, we needed a new home base — and we've found it at PHOOD Farm, who have been bitcoin-minded for years and accept sats as payment.

Food during the meetup. With enough signups, the venue will prepare a broth meal. Choose between chicken broth (homemade chicken bouillon with vegetables and toppings) or vegan broth (fully plant-based, with tempeh and vegetables). €22,50 per person.

It isn't a regular restaurant, so we need at least 20 diners to make the meal happen. Want to join? Let us know well in advance via a reply on Meetup or a message on Telegram.

See you on 12 June.
